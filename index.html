Integration notes

  • Integration is total stacked time at 10–20 s subs. Start at the low end; go to the high end (or past it) for faint arms, tails, halos and outer nebulosity.
  • Filter tags: UV/IR = the standard UV/IR-cut filter (galaxies, reflection nebulae, clusters). Dual-band = the built-in Ha/OIII filter (emission nebulae, planetaries, supernova remnants), which also cuts moonlight and light pollution.
  • Framing tags compare each target with the selected scope's field: Fits (any rotation), Fits lengthwise (fits when the field's long side lines up, which changes through the night in alt-az mode), Small (fits easily; crop afterward), or the mosaic size needed. Seestar's mosaic mode can capture those.
  • Integration times are set for the S30 Pro.
  • Split long totals across several nights in the same dark window and stack them. Battery is about 6 h, so plan one or two targets per night.
  • Targets tagged “near zenith” climb past 80°, where alt-az tracking struggles. Image them a couple of hours either side of transit, or use EQ mode on a wedge.
  • Amber “low” targets never clear 30° from this location. Image them within an hour or so of transit with a clean horizon in their direction.
  • Greyed-out targets never rise high enough to image from this location.

Season = months averaging at least half of the target's best-night dark hours. Peak = the night the target is up longest in full darkness. Dates are local calendar nights, labeled by the evening date. Sun and Moon use low-precision formulas, good to a few minutes. Positions J2000; sizes and magnitudes are approximate.
